Legal Basement Apartments

Turning a basement into a legal apartment involves more than finishing the space — layout, egress, ceiling height, and separation all factor into the plan from the start. We coordinate the construction alongside the permit process and design for long-term usability, not just passing inspection once.

  • Basement conversions and new apartment construction
  • Layout and permit-related planning
  • Long-term usability and livability

Permit approval and timelines are set by the municipality, not by us. We coordinate the process closely, but we can't guarantee approval or a specific date.
